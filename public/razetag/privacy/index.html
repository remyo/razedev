1. Information in the app

This policy covers RazeTag’s current local-first app and its promotional website and guide. It does not describe planned features that have not been released.

RazeTag saves the information you enter or import so you can manage inventory and track your business. This can include:

  • Product names, photos, descriptions, categories, quantities, barcodes, serial numbers, condition, and storage locations.
  • Purchase costs, selling prices, fees, budgets, sales, payments, refunds, and transaction history.
  • Notes and any seller, buyer, order, delivery, or contact details you choose to enter.
  • Reminder preferences, saved barcode matches, and records of changes or device syncing.

Your main records and app-managed photos are stored on your device, not in a RazeTag cloud account. They are used to provide the features you choose. This version has no RazeTag sign-in or advertising features. Avoid entering another person’s sensitive information unless you have a valid reason and permission to do so.

2. Camera, photos & recognition

The camera and selected photos are used to add product pictures, read labels and card text, and scan barcodes or QR codes. Recognition and photo editing run on the device; RazeTag does not upload your camera frames or personal photos to a cloud recognition service.

Some recognition and translation features use Google ML Kit. Google states that the images, video, and text processed by these APIs, and their recognition results, stay on-device. However, the SDK contacts Google for models, updates, and technical metrics. These can include device and app information, installation identifiers, feature usage, performance, configuration, and errors. Translation also involves language settings and model downloads. These diagnostics are separate from your inventory records.

Read Google’s ML Kit privacy explanation, its iOS disclosures and Android disclosures, and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Optional online services

Everyday inventory tracking works offline. The following features need internet access when used:

  • Card catalog search: TCGdex receives card identifiers and metadata needed for a lookup, such as set, card number, language, or illustrator, and supplies catalog information and card artwork. This can include details recognized from a card, but not your personal product photo or full inventory.
  • Exchange rates: Frankfurter receives currency requests to provide rates. RazeTag calculates the converted amount locally; the request does not send your inventory or entered purchase amount.
  • External links: a website or tracking link you open receives the usual browser request information and handles it under its own policy.

Online providers and their infrastructure can receive your IP address, request details, and other connection metadata. They may retain technical logs under their own practices. These services are distinct from local device sync and do not provide a RazeTag cloud backup.

4. Local sync, sharing & backups

Local sync connects devices over the same Wi-Fi after pairing and approval. It can transfer inventory, saved photos, purchase costs, expenses, sales, payments, and related history. Use it only with devices and people you trust. This is not anonymous public listing sharing.

Nearby item sharing sends the selected records to the recipient you approve. Purchase and private fields are limited by default, but shared records can still contain listing and transaction information. Review the selection and privacy options before sending.

Shop team is a separate, permission-based connection. The owner approves a member device and role. The downloaded selling catalog currently has no product photos; purchase costs are omitted unless the owner allows them. Membership, permissions, and pending-action records are kept locally. The agreed offline policy can lock access and optionally remove the downloaded catalog; it does not erase personal inventory, owner records, or previously exported copies. A disconnected device receives revocation or policy changes on its next authenticated connection.

Showcase displays the products you select on your own device. Its customer view excludes private purchase fields; it does not publish a website or prevent screenshots. Review product photos and public custom fields before showing them to someone.

Exports and copies are created when you ask. Backup files can contain inventory, history, and photos; spreadsheets and copied listings contain the details included in that export. When you save or share them through another app, file provider, cloud drive, or device, that destination controls its copy. Exported photos may retain metadata.

Encryption is optional for backup files. Enable encrypted backup and set a password if you need it. Spreadsheet exports are readable files, even when encrypted backup is enabled. Sync is not a replacement for a separate backup.

Your operating system may also back up app data according to your device settings. RazeTag does not control those system backups.

5. Device permissions

Permissions depend on your device and the feature you use. You can review or revoke them in your device’s settings; the related feature may then stop working.

Camera
Take item photos and read labels, cards, barcodes, or QR codes.
Photos & files
Choose pictures, save photo copies, and import or export backups and reports. Older Android versions may request storage access to save pictures.
Local network
Find nearby RazeTag devices and transfer data during sharing or sync. Previously synced devices may be discoverable while the app is open.
Notifications
Show locally scheduled reminders. Reminder text may be visible on your lock screen depending on your device settings.
Device authentication
Use the device’s supported authentication to protect selected app actions. RazeTag receives the authentication result, not your fingerprint or face template.
Internet access
Fetch optional catalog data, artwork, exchange rates, and recognition models, and allow the SDK communications explained above.

6. Keeping, correcting & deleting data

Local records remain until you change or delete them, clear the app’s data, or the operating system removes them. You can edit your saved information and export a copy using the app’s available tools.

  • Archive is not deletion. To permanently remove an archived listing, use the delete option in Archive and confirm.
  • Sold records: use Delete sold data in Settings to review and permanently delete selected records. Review carefully: this also affects the related history and reports.
  • Remembered barcodes: a learned barcode-to-product match can remain after its listing is deleted, to help fill in a future item. Removing a listing does not clear these saved suggestions.
  • Storage cleanup: clearing a cache removes rebuildable or temporary files, not your whole inventory.
  • Copies elsewhere: remove exported files, photo-library copies, system backups, and copies on other devices separately. Deleting a record here does not recall a file you already sent.

Removing all app data through your device or uninstalling where the operating system removes app storage can remove your local records and learned suggestions. System backups and separately saved files may remain. Keep a backup first if you intend to retain your records.

Because RazeTag has no server copy of your local inventory, the developer cannot remotely retrieve, correct, or erase it for you. Third-party service logs follow those providers’ retention practices.

7. Protecting your records

RazeTag uses the device’s app storage, optional device authentication, and encrypted local transfers. Online API requests use HTTPS. These safeguards do not mean every local database or export is separately encrypted by RazeTag.

Keep your device locked and updated, protect backup passwords, use trusted networks and recipients, and avoid sharing files that contain private purchase or contact details. No storage or transfer method can guarantee absolute security.

8. This website

The RazeTag landing page and guide do not connect to your app inventory. They contain no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or contact forms. App pictures in the guide use demonstration records.

The public site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub can process connection and security information, including IP addresses and request logs, when you visit. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to a service governed by its own privacy practices.

9. Children & policy changes

RazeTag is an inventory and resale tool for sellers and collectors, not a service designed for children. If a child’s personal information has been shared with the developer, use the privacy contact below so the concern can be addressed.

This page will be updated when the app’s data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. New features such as online accounts or cloud sync would need their own disclosures; they are not implied by this policy.

10. Privacy & support contact

For privacy questions, requests, or help with RazeTag, contact the RazeTag developer at razedevworkspace@gmail.com.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you include. We use that information to respond and resolve your request, and retain correspondence only as needed for support follow-up or legal obligations. You can ask us to delete support correspondence. This does not give us access to your device’s inventory.

Please include only the information needed to explain the issue. Do not send passwords or pairing codes, or post inventory backups or personal contact details in public comments or issue reports.
