From “I’ll note it later” to saved

Start small.
One item is enough.

You don’t need to set up a whole business first. Save the essentials, then fill in the rest when you’re ready.

  1. Choose how to add it

    On Home, tap + for a manual item or a photo. Use the scan icon for a barcode or QR code. You can also start from the Add tab.

  2. Give it a name and a cost

    Choose a category, add the quantity, and enter the buying price per piece. Photos and an optional selling target make it easier to find later.

  3. Save now. Update as you go.

    Find it in Inventory when you’re ready to edit, restock, or record a sale. Choose only the quantity that actually sold.

8 controllers at ₱600 each? Enter quantity 8 and unit cost ₱600. That’s one listing with ₱4,800 in buying cost—not eight duplicate listings.

Nearby devices, connected

Your inventory.
A little more in sync.

Work between your phone and another phone or Mac over the same Wi-Fi. You choose when to connect and approve the session.

  • Same Wi-Fi
  • Two devices
  • Both apps open

  1. Open Local network

    On both devices, go to Settings → Local network → Sync devices. Make a separate backup first.

  2. Start, join, approve

    Read the notice, start on one device, and join by QR or nearby-device code. Use the code on Mac.

  3. Keep working nearby

    Keep both apps open and devices awake. Review conflicting edits and deletions before confirming them.

Ordinary sync is for devices you trust. It includes private buying costs and financial history. It isn’t cloud or background sync, and it doesn’t replace a backup.

Just passing a few items along?

Share selected items nearby.

Choose Send on one device and Receive on the other. Select the products and review the private-information options before sending. This is a one-time handoff—not an ongoing sync of the whole inventory.

Settings → Local network → Send / Receive

Working with someone else?

Use Shop team for roles—not full sync.

The owner can invite a Manager or Staff device and choose its permissions. A member sees a limited selling catalog, currently without photos. Confirming actions requires both apps open on the same Wi-Fi, with one owner–member connection at a time.

Good questions

Before your
first find.

What matters when you’re getting started.

Is RazeTag only for trading cards?

No. Use it for consoles, games, controllers, figures, and other products. Add custom categories and fields in Settings → Categories when the defaults don’t fit your stock.

Can I use it without the internet?

Yes for everyday inventory, sales, and local reports. Optional online card references and fresh exchange rates need internet. Sharing and sync need both devices on the same local Wi-Fi—not a cloud account.

What if I bought several things as one bundle?

Choose Add → Purchase a bundle. Enter the total paid, add the items inside, and choose Equal, Resale value, or Manual to share the cost. Each child item stays searchable and can be sold separately.

What if only part of my stock sells?

Open the item → Mark as sold and enter the quantity sold. If you have eight and sell three, the other five stay in stock. The sale keeps its own quantity and financial record.

Can a customer browse without seeing my costs?

Use Tools → Showcase and choose the products to display. You control selling-price and detail visibility. The customer view excludes private purchase information; it isn’t an online storefront. Use its lock when handing over the device, and verify the view first.

Will sync keep my data safe if I lose my phone?

Sync isn’t a backup. Make a full backup from Settings → Backup & reports and keep it somewhere safe. Reports, screenshots, and copied listings don’t contain everything needed to restore your inventory.
