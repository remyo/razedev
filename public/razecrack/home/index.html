02 / A SPACE OF YOUR OWN

Less lost in tabs.
More saved for you.

Your saved videos deserve a proper home. Choose a view, give a video a familiar name, and pick up where you left off. Import photos and videos from your phone, too, while keeping the originals in Gallery.

  • Your Library, your view

    Grid or List, with your choice remembered in Library and Hidden. Filter by source or sort, alongside Search, Favorites, and Collections.

  • A name that makes sense to you

    Rename saved videos locally, search either title, and Restore original whenever you choose. The file and source details stay intact.

  • Easier to recognize

    Automatic thumbnails use source artwork or a frame from the saved video when available. Cached images stay ready offline.

  • Watch at your pace

    Choose 0.5×–2× playback, go fullscreen, or use 15-second skips. Picture in Picture is available on supported devices.

  • Share when you choose

    Open your phone’s chooser, or save a separate copy to Gallery. You make the call.
