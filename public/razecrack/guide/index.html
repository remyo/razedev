THE QUICK START

From link to
Library.

A few taps to keep a good find close. Here’s how to save, organize, and watch with Raze Crack.

Let’s get started ↓

FOUR SMALL STEPS

Your next watch
starts here.

  1. 01

    Find something worth keeping.

    Copy a public video link from a platform or website. Save only videos you own or have permission to download. You can paste the URL itself or a message containing links.

  2. 02

    Paste it on Home.

    Tap Paste to check a single valid link automatically. Several unique links open Choose videos, where you can select videos and qualities. You can also enter a link and tap Find video. Optional Auto paste only fills the field; you still choose when to find the video.

  3. 03

    Choose your format.

    Choose from the formats returned for that video, then tap Download video, or Add selected for several links. Available quality varies by source. Formats labeled Video only do not include audio.

  4. 04

    Make it part of your Library.

    Starting from Home keeps you there, with a progress card you can tap to open Downloads. Manage the current transfer and waiting queue there. Once the file is complete and validated, it appears in Library, ready to watch offline.

Download only what you own or have permission to save, and follow the source service’s terms.

ONCE YOU’RE IN

A home for
your good finds.

Keep the queue moving, put things in their place, and pick up where you left off.

Your queue, your order.

Tap Home’s progress card to open Downloads. Pause or resume the current transfer; a paused current item holds the queue. Prioritize moves a waiting video to the front and pauses the current transfer, without resuming unrelated paused items. Switch waiting items between List and Card views. Cancel asks for confirmation; failed downloads offer Try again.

Stay informed while you’re away.

Run in background in Settings controls downloading when you leave the app or lock your phone. Android shows measured progress in its ongoing notification. Notification permission is separate: denying it does not prevent downloads. Tap a public video’s completion notice to open Library, clear its view filters, find that video, and open its details. Progress and failure notices open Downloads. Hidden titles stay private.

A link from another app?

Share it to Raze Crack and choose Download now or Download later. Download now authorizes checking and adding the link while the app is open. Download later saves it in Shared links until you choose Download all or select links to download. Those actions open Downloads after the queue accepts the links; failed links keep their reason and a separate Retry action.

Settle in for another watch.

Open a saved video in the native player. Use the timeline and 15-second controls, or swipe up and down through your visible Library. Tap the video to show or hide controls; use Play/Pause to change playback. Fullscreen turns landscape videos sideways and keeps portrait videos upright. Picture in Picture is available on supported devices; Auto Picture-in-Picture is controlled in Settings.

Choose your pace.

Tap 1× in the player for speeds from 0.5× to 2×. Your choice stays with that session while swiping videos, entering fullscreen, or restoring Picture in Picture. Changing speed while paused keeps it paused. A new player session begins at 1×.

A view that feels right.

Choose Grid or List above All videos; Library and Hidden remember the same view preference. Filter holds sorting and the sources represented in your saved videos. Search, Favorites, and Collections work with those choices. Automatic thumbnails use cached source artwork or a frame from the saved video when available, with older videos handled as they come into view.

Give a video a familiar name.

Open its details and tap the pencil for Rename video. The local name appears in Library, Continue watching, and the player; Search finds both the new and original titles. Use Restore original to remove your custom name. Renaming keeps the saved file, original title and caption, and source link intact.

Bring your own photos and videos.

Tap Import in Library or Hidden and choose media in the system picker. Copies go into your chosen destination while the originals stay in Gallery. Keep the app open during copying; leaving stops the remaining imports and keeps completed copies. When photos are present, use Videos and Photos to browse each kind. Photos open in their own viewer.

Keep a little order.

Favorite videos and group them in named Collections. Search, source filters, and sorting work together. Hidden keeps selected videos out of the main Library and offers optional phone-lock protection; it does not encrypt files. Hidden videos stay out of Continue watching and cannot use Picture in Picture. Deleting a Collection removes the grouping and keeps its videos.

Save a copy to Gallery.

Choose Save to Gallery from a completed video, or select several in Library. The app keeps its private originals. Gallery copies stay separate, including after Library deletion or app uninstall. Export anything you want to keep before uninstalling.

A FEW ANSWERS

Good to know.

Why can’t I download a particular video?+

Availability depends on the video, website, region, and available format. A public link is not a guarantee that a video can be downloaded. Raze Crack does not sign in to other platforms or remove login, age-verification, private-content, or protected-media restrictions. Read the specific error shown in the app before trying again.

Will a paused download always resume from the same place?+

Saved partial data is reused when the source and transfer support it. Expired links, missing temporary data, or changes at the source can mean some bytes must be downloaded again. Resume is not guaranteed for every video.

Will downloads keep running when I leave the app?+

Run in background defaults on. On Android, the native download service shows progress in an ongoing notification. Turn the setting off to pause work when the app leaves the foreground or the phone locks; returning resumes only work paused by that setting. Manually paused items stay paused. The operating system, connection and source can interrupt work, and continuation after force-stop is not guaranteed.

Can I download a video on this website?+

Downloading happens in the installed Raze Crack Android app. This website explains the app and provides help; it does not process video links or save videos.

Why does a saved video still have a placeholder thumbnail?+

Raze Crack caches valid source artwork or tries a frame from the saved video. Missing artwork and unsupported or damaged media can leave a placeholder. On Android 7.0–8.0, larger videos may keep a placeholder when source artwork fails because scaled frame extraction is unavailable. Cached thumbnails are reused offline and recreated as needed if the system clears them.

What happens when I remove a video or uninstall the app?+

Remove from Library deletes the app’s private saved file. Copies you have explicitly saved to Gallery remain separate and are not removed with it. Uninstalling removes the app’s private Library, so save any videos you want to keep to Gallery first.

Does sharing send a video automatically?+

No. Share opens your phone’s native sharing chooser with the saved video. You choose the destination and whether to continue. Save to Gallery is also a manual action.
