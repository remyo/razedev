THE QUICK START

From link to
Library.

A few taps to keep a good find close. Here’s how to save, organize, and watch with Raze Crack.

Let’s get started ↓

FOUR SMALL STEPS

Your next watch
starts here.

  1. 01

    Find something worth keeping.

    Copy a public video link you own or have permission to save. You can paste the URL itself or a message containing it.

  2. 02

    Paste it on Home.

    Tap Paste to check a single valid link automatically. You can also enter a link and tap Find video. Optional Auto paste only fills the field; you still choose when to find the video.

  3. 03

    Choose your format.

    Choose from the formats returned for that video, then tap Download. Available quality varies by source. Formats labeled Video only do not include audio.

  4. 04

    Make it part of your Library.

    Follow progress in Downloads, where you can manage the current transfer and waiting queue. Once complete, your saved video appears in Library, ready for another watch.

Download only what you own or have permission to save, and follow the source service’s terms.

ONCE YOU’RE IN

A home for
your good finds.

Keep the queue moving, put things in their place, and pick up where you left off.

Your queue, your order.

Pause or resume the current download in Downloads. A paused current item holds the queue. Prioritize moves a waiting video to the front without resuming unrelated paused items. Switch waiting items between List and Card views.

A link from another app?

Share it to Raze Crack and choose Download now or Download later. Download now authorizes checking and adding the link while the app is open. Download later saves it in Shared links until you choose Download all or select links to download.

Settle in for another watch.

Open a saved video in the fullscreen native player. Use the timeline and 15-second controls, or swipe up and down through your visible Library. Picture in Picture is available on supported devices; Auto Picture-in-Picture is controlled in Settings.

Keep a little order.

Favorite videos and group them in named Collections. Search, source filters, and sorting work together. Hidden keeps selected videos out of the main Library and offers optional phone-lock protection. Deleting a Collection removes the grouping and keeps its videos.

Save a copy to Gallery.

Choose Save to Gallery from a completed video, or select several in Library. The app keeps its private originals. Gallery copies stay separate, including after Library deletion or app uninstall. Export anything you want to keep before uninstalling.

A FEW ANSWERS

Good to know.

Why can’t I download a particular video?+

Availability depends on the video, website, region, and available format. A public link is not a guarantee that a video can be downloaded. Raze Crack does not sign in to other platforms or remove login, age-verification, private-content, or protected-media restrictions. Read the specific error shown in the app before trying again.

Will a paused download always resume from the same place?+

Saved partial data is reused when the source and transfer support it. Expired links, missing temporary data, or changes at the source can mean some bytes must be downloaded again. Resume is not guaranteed for every video.

Can I download a video on this website?+

Downloading happens in the installed Raze Crack mobile app. This website explains the app and provides help; it does not process video links or save videos.

What happens when I remove a video or uninstall the app?+

Remove from Library deletes the app’s private saved file. Copies you have explicitly saved to Gallery remain separate and are not removed with it. Uninstalling removes the app’s private Library, so save any videos you want to keep to Gallery first.

Does sharing send a video automatically?+

No. Share opens your phone’s native sharing chooser with the saved video. You choose the destination and whether to continue. Save to Gallery is also a manual action.
