A HELPFUL FIRST NOTE

A few details
go a long way.

For a bug or download issue, please include:

  • Your device model and Android version.
  • The Raze Crack app version, shown in Settings.
  • What you were trying to do and the exact error message.
  • A public, non-sensitive source URL, if it helps explain the issue.

Please leave out passwords, cookies, private or signed links, and other sensitive information. You don’t need to send the video file by default.
