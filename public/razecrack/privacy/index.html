A QUICK READ

Your files have a local home.

Raze Crack stores your Library on your phone. It has no Raze account, developer-operated cloud Library, advertising SDK, or developer analytics SDK in the current Android implementation. Source websites still receive requests when you look up videos, download files, or display remote thumbnails.

This policy describes the Raze Crack Android app provided by Raze Dev. Contact razeappdev@gmail.com about privacy or support.

01

Information on your device

The app stores downloaded videos and related information in private app storage: original source links, available titles and captions, thumbnail URLs, download status, and supported transfer checkpoints. It also stores your link draft, incoming shared links, settings, favorites, collections, Hidden status and lock preferences, playback progress, and records of completed Gallery exports.

These records support downloads, queue recovery, organizing your Library, playback, and exports you request. Source links and private transfer information may contain signed URL parameters. The app does not provide a Raze user account or a developer-operated cloud Library.

02

Clipboard & shared links

Tapping Paste reads clipboard text to find video links and can start the lookup you request. Auto paste is optional and initially off. When enabled, it checks new clipboard text while Home is active and fills the link field. Auto paste does not automatically inspect or download those links; you can turn it off in Settings.

Links shared to Raze Crack are saved locally. Download later keeps them for later action. Choosing Download now authorizes inspection and queueing while the main app is active. Sharing a link alone does not start a download.

03

Connections to websites

Requested lookups and downloads connect directly to source websites, media hosts, and related endpoints. Those services receive information inherent in the request, including your IP address, the requested URL and its parameters, and headers such as User-Agent and Referer. Displaying remote thumbnails, including in saved items, also contacts their hosts.

Some public pages require an isolated Android WebView to render their contents. Page scripts can make additional requests and process information under the source service’s policies. The app does not import your browser login session or sign you in. Its separate WebView profile is cleared between attempts, and third-party cookies are disabled. This does not mean websites receive no information.

Source services control their own data handling and retention. The current app has no developer-operated extraction backend, no advertising or developer analytics SDK, and no automatic developer crash-report upload. It may write bounded diagnostic messages to the device’s system log.

04

Sharing & Gallery copies

Share opens Android’s native chooser. The receiving app you choose can access the selected saved video. Save to Gallery makes a separate copy in Movies/Raze Crack in shared media storage. These actions are manual; the app does not automatically share or export your videos.

Opening an original source link uses your phone’s external handler, which may open a browser or the source app. Receiving apps, websites, Gallery services, and any enabled photo-backup service apply their own policies. A copy you export or share remains separate from the private Library.

05

Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: lookups, thumbnails, and downloads.
  • Foreground service, wake lock, and notifications: ongoing transfers and visible progress; notification permission may be requested where Android requires it.
  • Legacy storage access on Android 9 and earlier: requested for manual Gallery export.
  • Device authentication: optional phone-lock protection for Hidden.

The current Android app does not request contacts, location, microphone, camera, or broad photo/video-library access.

06

Security & Hidden

Private files use Android’s app-storage protections. HTTPS requests use transport encryption where supported. The app also accepts HTTP links and permits cleartext traffic, so not every possible connection is encrypted. No promise of end-to-end encryption or separate encryption of local records is made.

Hidden can use your phone’s screen-lock authentication. The app receives the authentication result, rather than your screen-lock credentials. Hidden controls visibility and access inside the app; it is not a separate encryption guarantee.

07

Retention & deletion

Private videos and their records remain until you remove them or clear the app’s data. You can remove videos through Library, remove saved shared links, clear the link field, manage removable download cache through Local storage, and remove collections. Deleting a collection keeps its videos. Cache cleanup protects saved files and active, queued, and paused work.

Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the private data from that installation. Gallery exports and shared copies remain outside the private Library and must be managed separately. Depending on Android and device settings, app data may be included in operating-system backup or device transfer. Manage those copies through the relevant provider.

Raze Dev cannot remotely erase files on your phone or copies retained by source websites, receiving apps, Gallery services, or backup providers. There is no Raze account to delete.

08

When you contact us

Emailing razeappdev@gmail.com sends your address, message, and any attachments you choose to provide to Raze Dev’s support mailbox and its email provider. They are used to read and respond to your request. Do not send passwords, browser cookies, private signed links, or video files unless a specific support need has been discussed.

Access to support emails and attachments is limited to Raze Dev’s support handling. We retain support emails and attachments for 12 months after the request is closed, then delete them. You can request earlier deletion at razeappdev@gmail.com.

Our email provider handles its backups separately under its own policies. Deleting a support email does not promise immediate deletion from the provider’s backups.

09

Age & intended audience

Raze Crack is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not intended for children. The app has no Raze account and does not ask for your name, date of birth, or age. Source websites still receive the network information described above.

Source services have their own age limits, content restrictions, and terms. Raze Crack does not remove those restrictions or make third-party content appropriate for children.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to the support mailbox, contact razeappdev@gmail.com to request review and deletion.

10

This website

This website does not add analytics, advertising, contact forms, cookies, or browser storage. Its fonts, artwork, and screenshots are served with the website. The app screenshots use example content; they are not your Library.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives ordinary web requests and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even if they are not signed in. GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement explain the provider’s handling. We do not control GitHub’s security-log retention. Email links open your chosen email app. No message is sent automatically.

11

Changes & contact

This policy is effective 23 October 2026. We will update it when the app or its data handling changes and show the revised effective date on this page.
